Frequently Asked Questions

How fast is the delivery?

For local villages, the store owner personally drives the delivery vehicle. Orders are typically fulfilled within 15 to 30 minutes depending on distance and road conditions.

What payment modes are supported?

We support Cash on Delivery (COD), UPI Instant Transfers (where you scan the QR and enter UTR), as well as secure checkout via Razorpay supporting cards and wallets.

What is the return policy?

If any grocery item (fruits, vegetables, or dairy products) arrives damaged or stale, please notify the owner within 24 hours of delivery for an instant replacement or refund.
